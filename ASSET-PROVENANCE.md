# Portfolio asset provenance

## Tool marks

Monochrome tool marks are locally vendored from Simple Icons v11.15.0
(https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) and Lobe Icons static SVG
(https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), through jsDelivr. Brand names and logos
belong to their owners. These marks illustrate a creative toolbox, not certification.

## Product presentations

`public/showcase/covers/` contains built-in ImageGen presentation edits made from
the supplied original notebooks, tote, mugs, ADCO clock and DEİK pin. Brief:
preserve product artwork; fully visible product; landscape black studio photograph;
controlled reflections; no new branding. The original artwork remains available
in each product's detail gallery. The car freshener was removed from the selection.

## Independent concepts

`public/showcase/concepts/` contains three explicitly fictional, AI-assisted product
visualizations, not client commissions or manufactured products:

- `selcuk-watch.webp`: eight-point geometric dial, silver watch case, dark teal strap,
  refined Turkish geometric surface treatment, full product on black studio backdrop.
- `tulip-porcelain.webp`: tulip-inspired porcelain espresso cups, cobalt lines,
  matching packaging, complete set on black studio backdrop.
- `anatolia-stationery.webp`: embossed Anatolian geometric notebook and brass
  bookmark, museum gift set, complete arrangement on black studio backdrop.

All nine cover/concept images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and
exported as WebP for the website. Visible labels distinguish concepts from client work.
